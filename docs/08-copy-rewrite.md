## Copy principles applied throughout
- Active voice, second person, benefit-led; every claim paired with a number.
- One idea per section; progressive disclosure over walls of text.
- No repeated blocks — stats, logos, CTAs and categories each defined once and reused as components.
- Consistent terminology: **profit-first**, **growth partner**, **two motions** (Launch & Growth / Scale & Optimize), **three pillars**.
